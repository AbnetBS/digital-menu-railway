# Store: setup and operating notes

1. Sign into `/admin` as the owner, then open `/store` (also linked in the dashboard). Under **Stock accepters**, create a **store manager**; managers can create named stock accepters. Each account signs in at `/store` with its own PIN. Managers can reset or remove accepter logins; only the owner can manage manager logins. Removing a login does not remove request history. Resetting a PIN invalidates old store sessions.
2. On an empty store, managers see three illustrated **examples**. Their quantities are not real and are never saved as inventory, requests, recipes or reports. Use **Remove examples** to hide them in that browser; they also disappear automatically when the first real item is added. Under **Store stock**, add each stock item once. Select liquid (ml/L), weight (g/kg), or counted pieces. Enter opening stock and a low-stock threshold, optionally with a photo. The threshold is in the store, not the stations. Later purchases use **Add stock** on the item. If buying two 5 L containers, enter 10 L.
3. On the kitchen, barista or juice dashboard, signed-in station staff can open **Station stock** to enter purchases or opening stock received directly by their own station. Choose an existing item or create a named item with unit, quantity and optional photo. A new item joins the shared store catalog; it does **not** add to the main store's balance. Direct purchases are reported separately, not as store issues. Station staff can also record waste from their own station. Buna stock is supplied through store requests.
4. Select **Ingredients**, open a menu card, and enter each stock ingredient needed for **one portion**. The available units follow the ingredient's type. A recipe change affects future completed lines only; it does not rewrite past usage. An item with no recipe is marked **Missing recipe** in the report; no usage can be calculated for it.
5. A stock accepter signs in, chooses **Kitchen**, **Barista**, **Juice** or **Buna** for each request (Kitchen is the default), adds one or more items and sends it. A request has one destination; make separate requests for different stations. The accepter can edit it while pending. The store checks availability on sending and again when the manager accepts. If that station already has any of the items, the manager must confirm checking its recorded stock before issuing more. Acceptance atomically reduces the store and increases the chosen station's balance.
6. **Station stock** shows theoretical balances, not physical counts. Filter by station or view all. When a station completes a ticket line, the current recipe and completed quantity are recorded once and deducted from that station in the same transaction. Kitchen, barista and juice normally press Done. Buna lines are completed on cashier print or delivery, or created completed when a coffee note is paid. Print followed by delivery does not deduct twice. A line already finished before the store is first opened is not backfilled. Setting an item back to pending and Done again does not deduct it a second time.
7. Record known spoilage, damage and other waste in **Station stock** with a reason, choosing either the store or the relevant station. Store **Reports** show completed items by station/category, ingredient use, waste reasons, direct station purchases, and recorded usage efficiency (recipe usage divided by recipe usage plus recorded waste). This is a theoretical indicator, not proof of actual efficiency: unreported loss, incomplete recipes, substitutions, staff meals and manual counts are not automatically captured. A negative balance signals a discrepancy and is deliberately not hidden. Completed items may not be paid sales; this report is separate from the financial admin report.
8. Under **Breakage / shortage**, a manager selects an attendance member, enters the item and its price, and records the deduction. The history shows the time and allows an incorrect entry to be voided, with its audit history retained. Active entries automatically appear in the employee's Payroll for the current Ethiopian month; Payroll no longer accepts manually entered breakage. Previously entered payroll breakage amounts are preserved as historical entries. An approved or paid month, and an employee marked paid, cannot be changed unless unlocked through the existing payroll controls. Review the applicable employment rules and obtain any required authorization before deducting pay.

## Important rollout limitations

- Configure opening store and station stock and enter/verify recipes **before relying on balances**. The system begins observing completed lines when `/store` is first opened; it does not import historical transactions or recreate previous station balances. Each store item is limited to liquid, weight or whole pieces; packaging sizes are not yet tracked separately.
- Changing quantities, cancelling or deleting a ticket after a line was completed does **not** reverse or alter the recorded usage. Completion indicates that preparation happened; corrections require physical investigation. A dedicated audited stock-adjustment/reconciliation workflow is not yet included. Do not treat the expected balance as an audited physical count.
- The low-stock warning is displayed in `/store`, not delivered as a phone push notification. A manager must keep the page open or revisit it to see requests and warnings. The page refreshes automatically approximately every 10 seconds.
- The store requires database permission to create its tables, indexes, function and trigger on first use. The trigger runs within the order transaction, so usage cannot be silently lost if a completed order commits. Test the rollout with a backup and a real database before relying on it; embedded PostgreSQL tests do not replace live Coolify/PostgreSQL migration, concurrency and integration tests.
